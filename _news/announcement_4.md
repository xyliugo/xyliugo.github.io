Our paper [NoisyRollout](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.13055) is accepted to NeurIPS 2025! NoisyRollout is a simple yet effective rollout-level data augmentation technique that improves visual reasoning in VLMs by mixing trajectories from clean and noisy images during RL training.
